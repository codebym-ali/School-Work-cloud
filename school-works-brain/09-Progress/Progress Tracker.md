## ➕ Business rule — one campus admin per campus (2026-07-21)
- [x] **A campus may have at most one `CAMPUS_ADMIN` (the principal).** Enforced in `UsersService` across all three assignment paths — `create` (`POST /users`), `update` (`PATCH /users/:id` role/campus change), and the grant (`PATCH /users/:id/campus-admin`) — via a shared `assertNoOtherCampusAdmin(campusId, exceptUserId?)` that 409s (`CONFLICT`) when another active (`deletedAt: null`) campus admin already holds that campus.
